# Final Integration Checklist

- [ ] Same raw dataset used or differences documented.
- [ ] Cleaning steps are reproducible; raw data remains unchanged.
- [ ] Every notebook runs from top to bottom.
- [ ] Graph titles, axes, units, legends, and sample sizes are clear.
- [ ] Numerical claims match notebook or CSV output.
- [ ] Interpretations do not claim causation from correlation.
- [ ] Recommendations link a finding to an action and measurable outcome.
- [ ] Teammate results replace PPT placeholders.
- [ ] PPT numbers and graphs match the reports.
- [ ] Presenters know their sections and handoffs.
- [ ] Full presentation is rehearsed and timed.
- [ ] Final files are backed up in two locations.
